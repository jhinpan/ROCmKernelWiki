## Summary (from upstream PR description)

This pr enables generation of swizzled tensors for mfma dot operands.
Related PR: binarman#4
Details
Swizzling pattern is defined by three values: perPhase, maxPhase, vecSize:
The goal of current strategy to maximize vecSize by minimizing maxPhase and perPhase values, while eliminating bank conflicts for mfma 32x32 instructions.
