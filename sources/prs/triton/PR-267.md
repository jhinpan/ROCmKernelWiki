# [FA] Keep Q tensor in registers.

**Repository:** [ROCm/triton](https://github.com/ROCm/triton) · **PR:** [#267](https://github.com/ROCm/triton/pull/267) · **Merged:** 2023-07-24 · **Author:** @oplavsic

**Inclusion reason:** keyword 'attention'

## Summary (from upstream PR description)

Enable moving shared->dot conversion after dependent load. For the Q tensor in flash attention, the shared->dot conversion acts as a loop invariant. Moving this conversion post dependent load will hoist the conversion outside the loop. Consequently, during computation, we will be able to maintain the Q tensor in the registers.
