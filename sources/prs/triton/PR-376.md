Notes

best perf configs for 1k, 2k, 4k, and 8k are added in the autotuning.
For 9728x8192x65536, will update the best config when tuning is done.
The tflops for 2k,4k, and 8k matches the results from gemm tuning script. However, tflops for 1k is lower (we have 65 from tuning script). This is because 1k is relative a small gemm and the overhead of do_bench() becomes non-negligible.
