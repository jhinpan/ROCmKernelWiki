# [TRITON] Add Group Matrix Multiplication (GMM) Triton kernels to AITER

**Repository:** [ROCm/aiter](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter) · **PR:** [#1072](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/1072) · **Merged:** 2025-10-01 · **Author:** @brunomazzottiamd

**Inclusion reason:** kernel path 'aiter/ops/triton/_triton_kernels/gmm.py'; keyword 'kernel'

## Summary (from upstream PR description)

Motivation
A client has requested this feature, and since we developed GMM Triton kernels in the scope of ROCm/triton-internal#716, we decided to integrate them into AITER.
Technical Details
There are more than one kernel in the PR, for supporting forward and backward.
In GMM variant, $lhs$ rows are divided into $G$ groups. Each group of $lhs$ rows is matrix multiplied with a plane of $rhs$ 3D tensor and then stored in a slice of $out$. In PyTorch parlance, it can be implemented as follows for a given group $g$: out[group_start:group_end, :] = lhs[group_start:group_end, :] @ rhs[g].
The size of each group, and their respective start and end positions are specified by group_sizes tensor. For instance, suppose that group_sizes = [3, 2, 4, 1]. In this particular case we have 4 groups. The 1st group starts at 0 and ends at 2, the second group starts at 3 and ends at 4, the third group starts at 5 and ends at 8, and the fourth and final group consists of just the 10th (last) row of $lhs$.
In TGMM variant, $lhs$ columns and $rhs$ rows are divided into $G$ groups. Each group of $lhs$ is matrix multiplied with the respective group of $rhs$ and then stored in a plane of the output 3D tensor
