## Summary (from upstream PR description)

Summary
Re-tuned DSv4 FP8 a8w8 blockscale bpreshuffle GEMM configs for gfx950 (MI355, 256 CUs). 43 shapes improved across all batch sizes (M=1-32768), primarily by switching from CK intrawave_v1 to ASM splitK kernels.
Before: all small/medium-M shapes used CK intrawave_v1 (~17-20 us regardless of shape). After: ASM splitK kernels achieve 1.1-1.9x speedup for memory-bound shapes.
