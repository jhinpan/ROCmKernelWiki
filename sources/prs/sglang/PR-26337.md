Motivation

On AMD gfx950, the FP8 block-scaled GEMM dispatch maintains a tuned list of (N, K) shapes that route to a Triton kernel (triton_gemm_a8w8_blockscale). Shapes not in this list fall through to the AITER Composable Kernel (CK) gemm_a8w8_blockscale_bpreshuffle backend.
The shape (7168, 2048) corresponds to the wo_b projection in DeepSeek-V4's MLA, which projects from the compressed latent space (n_groups × o_lora_rank, TP-sharded) back to the hidden dimension. This shape was included in the Triton tuned list, but profiling shows that the CK bpreshuffle kernel is faster for this particular shape.
