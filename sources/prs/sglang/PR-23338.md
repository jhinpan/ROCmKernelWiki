## Summary (from upstream PR description)

Add !cancelled() guard to first-wave AMD CI jobs (sgl-kernel, jit-kernel, stage-a, multimodal) so they respect workflow cancellation.
These jobs used always() without !cancelled(), causing them to remain stuck in "Waiting for a runner" even after the workflow was manually cancelled.
